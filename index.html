Brand Story

Not just raw materials. A place, a craft, a story.

Nepal remains a rare origin in global coffee and tea markets. Its value lives in more than volume: Himalayan nature, altitude, careful handwork, and the human stories of small-scale farming communities.

Himalayan Origin presents Nepalese coffee and Ilam tea as premium brand experiences for partners who want provenance, restraint, and a meaningful sourcing narrative.

Coffee

Nepal Specialty Coffee

Nepalese coffee is still rare in the global specialty market. Grown in the mid-hill regions of Nepal at approximately 1,250-1,500m altitude, our coffee offers a unique origin story for specialty roasters, boutique cafes, sustainable brands, and ethical retailers.

Ilam Tea & Botanical Blends

Ilam, located near the Darjeeling region, is one of Nepal's most respected tea-growing areas. Its geography, climate, and tea culture make it suitable for premium orthodox tea, wellness blends, and hospitality experiences.

Sustainability

Ethical Sourcing, Human Stories

We aim to build long-term relationships with farming communities, sourcing partners, and buyers who care about transparency. Our approach is grounded in fairer trading conversations, farmer support, environmental consideration, and honest origin storytelling.

Every lot should carry context: where it came from, who helped produce it, and why the origin matters. For premium partners, that transparency becomes part of the experience.
